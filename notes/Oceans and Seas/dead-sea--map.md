## Back

<b>Dead Sea</b>

## Extra

Its shores, 439.78 m below sea level (2025), are the lowest land-based elevation on Earth<br>304 m deep, the deepest hypersaline lake in the world

About 9.6 times as salty as the ocean (34.2% in 2011), so swimming is like floating. Plants and animals cannot flourish in it, hence the name.
