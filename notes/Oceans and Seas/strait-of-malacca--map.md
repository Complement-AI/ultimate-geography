## Back

<b>Strait of Malacca</b>

## Extra

About 900 km long, 65 to 250 km wide<br>Between the Malay Peninsula and Sumatra

Connects the Andaman Sea (Indian Ocean) and the South China Sea (Pacific). Over 94,000 vessels a year (2008) made it the busiest strait in the world, carrying about 25% of traded goods.
