<b>Yellow Sea</b>

## Extra

About 380,000 km², 44 m deep on average<br>Between mainland China and the Korean Peninsula

One of four seas named after a colour. Its name describes the golden-yellow of the silt that rivers such as the Yellow River pour into it.
