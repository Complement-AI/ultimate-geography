Adopted in 1971, shortly before independence

Maroon with a white band of nine points at the hoist; the points represent Qatar as the 9th "reconciled emirate". It is the only national flag more than twice as wide as it is tall.

Similar flags: Bahrain (narrower, fewer serrated edges, red)
