Adopted on 22 July 1947, in use from 15 August 1947

Saffron, white and green with a navy blue 24-spoke wheel, the Ashoka Chakra. In 1947 Nehru replaced the charkha, a spinning wheel, from the earlier Swaraj flag with the chakra.
