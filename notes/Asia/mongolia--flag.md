Adopted on 10 July 1945; the communist star above the symbol was removed in 1992

The blue stripe is the eternal blue sky. The Soyombo symbol at the hoist combines fire, sun, moon, earth and water with a pair of fish, which never sleep, like the spirit of the Mongol people.
