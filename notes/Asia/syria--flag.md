First used as the independence flag in the First and Second Syrian Republics

The green stripe represented the Rashidun Caliphate, the white the Umayyad Caliphate and the black the Abbasid Caliphate. The three red stars originally stood for the states of Aleppo, Damascus and Deir ez-Zor.
