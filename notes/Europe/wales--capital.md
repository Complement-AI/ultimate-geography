## Front

What is the capital of <b>Wales</b>?

## Back

<b>Cardiff</b>

## Extra

About 382,000 people (2025)<br>In the southeast of Wales

Only proclaimed capital in 1955. A small town until the early 19th century, it grew as a coal port; Caerdydd comes from words meaning "the fort of the Taff".

Constituent country of the United Kingdom.
