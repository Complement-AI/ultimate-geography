## Front

What is the capital of <b>Northern Ireland</b>?

## Back

<b>Belfast</b>

## Extra

About 345,000 people (2021), the largest city in Northern Ireland<br>On the banks of the river Lagan, near Belfast Lough

The name comes from the Irish Béal Feirste. It became the capital of the six north-eastern counties in 1920–22, and by the 1900s its shipyards were building up to a quarter of all United Kingdom tonnage.

Constituent country of the United Kingdom.
