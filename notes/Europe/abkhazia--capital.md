## Front

What is the capital of <b>Abkhazia</b>?

## Back

<b>Sukhumi</b>

## Extra

About 65,000 people (2022)<br>In a wide bay on the Black Sea's eastern coast

Greeks settled it in the 6th century BC and named it Dioscurias. The modern name is supposed to derive from a Svan word for the hornbeam tree.

Independent state claimed by Georgia.
