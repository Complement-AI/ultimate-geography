## Front

What is the capital of <b>South Ossetia</b>?

## Back

<b>Tskhinvali</b>

## Extra

About 32,700 people (2020)<br>On the Great Liakhvi River, about 100 km northwest of Tbilisi

Also called Tskhinval, and occasionally Stalinir. Its status is disputed: it is the capital of the de facto independent Republic of South Ossetia, which Georgia and most states regard as part of Georgia.

Independent state claimed by Georgia.
