## Front

<b>Tiraspol</b> is the capital of which country or territory?

## Back

<b>Transnistria</b>

## Extra

About 368,000 people (2024) in 4,163 km²<br>A landlocked breakaway state, internationally recognised as part of Moldova

Officially the Pridnestrovian Moldavian Republic. A military conflict with Moldova began in March 1992 and ended with a ceasefire that July, which has held.

Independent state claimed by Moldova.
